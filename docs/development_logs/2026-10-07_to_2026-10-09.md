## 2026-10-07 17:12 — Remediate live evidence and rule/report findings

### Objective

Execute the saved TAVIDM `implementation` prompt by fixing supported scan findings, adding focused regression coverage, and recording blockers that require product or schema decisions.

---

### Summary

No-entry evaluation now uses the saved prohibited side, an explicit prohibited vector, or an unambiguous associated lane flow; it requires a unique threshold linked to the sign's lane and reports missing or ambiguous context. Live review rows persist confidence and factor evidence, while the live monitor surfaces Truck-Ban clock and No Side Mirror visibility gates. ---

### Detailed Changes

No-entry now requires exactly one threshold with lane IDs intersecting the sign's lane IDs. It uses a saved `prohibited_from` side directly, preserves valid `prohibited_vector` behavior, or derives prohibited movement opposite one consistent lane-flow vector. Missing or conflicting context produces a diagnostic and no candidate. The existing review-only outcome remains unchanged.

Tracker history is pruned by elapsed seconds with a ten-second retention window, exceeding the current nine-second parking smoothing/comparison need. Equal timestamps replace the latest sample so duplicate media timestamps cannot grow the buffer without bound. Integrated tests show stationary candidates at 15/30/60 Hz and reject sustained movement at 60 Hz.

Live candidates now persist the same dual-confidence and factor fields as uploaded-video candidates. Live status reports unavailable capture time and mirror mounting visibility, and the Live Monitor displays those diagnostics. Processing-run diagnostics also capture rule diagnostics discovered during frame evaluation.

Report rows now render every contributing behavior with its own category and legal status. The PDF wraps both policy association columns. The 5,000-row cap remains unchanged pending an export policy decision.
