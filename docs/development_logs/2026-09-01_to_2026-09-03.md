## 2026-09-01 00:32 — Diagnose Pickup-Classification Failure

### Objective

Determine why the completed Vehicle PH v5 pickup run produced near-zero pickup-truck validation metrics without changing datasets, checkpoints, or training state.

---

### Summary

Added a read-only checkpoint comparison that matches predictions from the predecessor and candidate weights against the exact pickup ground-truth boxes. The generated validation and test reports show that the candidate generally detects the vehicles but classifies them primarily as `car` or `van`, and that each benchmark split's pickup examples come from only one source video.

---

### Files Modified

#### Created

- `scripts/diagnose_pickup_regression.py`
  - Compares two YOLO checkpoints at low confidence and reports pickup overlap, competing classes, source counts, and bounding-box geometry.
- `diagnostics/pickup_v5_regression_2026-09-01.json`
  - Validation-split evidence for all 69 pickup annotations.
- `diagnostics/pickup_v5_test_regression_2026-09-01.json`
  - Test-split evidence for all 51 pickup annotations.
- `docs/development_logs/2026-09-01_to_2026-09-03.md`
  - Records this completed diagnostic task.

---

### Detailed Changes

- Enforced the expected ten-class checkpoint roster before comparison.
- Measured the best overlapping prediction of any class and the best pickup prediction for each pickup ground-truth box.
- Reported train/evaluation source composition and pickup-box geometry distributions.
- Preserved all source datasets, caches, checkpoints, and training results unchanged.

---

### Technical Explanation

The probe runs inference at confidence `0.001`, matches predictions to pickup annotations by intersection-over-union, and separately counts pickup matches at IoU 0.50 and confidence 0.25. This distinguishes missed localization from class confusion while avoiding mutation of Ultralytics dataset caches.

---

### Code Impact

None. The script and reports are diagnostic artifacts and are not imported by the application or training pipeline.

- Test probe completed on 51 pickup annotations from 43 images.
- The standard Ultralytics test validator was not used because its stale-cache recovery attempted to rewrite the protected test cache; the read-only matcher was used instead.

---

### Performance Notes

The focused comparison evaluates only images containing pickup annotations, reducing GPU work relative to full-split validation.

### Known Limitations
